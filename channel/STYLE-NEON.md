# Neon style: analysis of the reference video

Reference: "YouTube Is Testing Your Channel Right Now" (6:28), uploaded to `main`.
Frame sheets: `images/neon-ref-1.jpg`, `-2`, `-3`.

## What the video does

1. **One big metaphor carries the whole video.** YouTube = an *audition*: theater
   curtains, a round stage, a spotlight, the character wears a contestant number "17",
   a crowd counter "559 AUDITIONS". The stage comes back at the start, the middle and
   the end. Every idea is explained inside this one world.
2. **Hybrid format:** ~70% neon animation, ~15% real screen captures (YouTube Studio,
   analytics graphs, a YouTube employee clip), ~15% host face-cam. For a faceless
   channel the character replaces the host.
3. **Only 5 hard cuts in 6.5 minutes.** The world never "cuts": elements slide in,
   transform in place, the camera pans. It feels like one continuous space.
4. **The brand is a character.** The YouTube play button has eyes and eyebrows (angry,
   curious) and talks in a speech bubble ("NO").
5. **Crowds = data.** Rows of small neon people in different colors (teal, yellow-green)
   show audiences, viewers and segments. A spotlight picks one out.
6. **Props tell the story:** a straw hat = gardening audience, a cap = car audience,
   a seedling box, a TV tower "broadcast OFF", a calendar, a phone.
7. **Text as animation:** numbered rules (RULE 1 on a pedestal, pills 1-2-3), kinetic
   headlines ("STOP JUDGING BY THE FIRST"), a red strikethrough over the myth
   ("~~BEAT THE ALGORITHM~~").
8. **Data shown by contrast:** tiny red bars (4, 12, 7) vs one huge gold bar (48,210)
   with a crown; a 10% click-through ring; a views curve.
9. **Ends on a question:** an empty stage with a big "?" and "WHO ARE YOU AUDITIONING?".

## Look

- Near-black green background, faint perspective floor grid, floating dust, vignette.
- Line-art neon characters: round head, dot eyes, one-line mouth, oval body, capsule
  limbs, soft floor shadow. Emotion = eyes + mouth + arm pose.
- Glass panels (dark green, rounded, thin mint border, glow); white pill labels with icons;
  icons in glowing circles.
- Colors: mint green (main), teal and lime (crowds), gold (win, money), red (YouTube, loss),
  white text. Bold geometric sans, big numbers.

## How we adapt it for The Money Trail (finance)

- **Main character:** a neon young man with a cap and a "$" badge on his chest (not a headset,
  so we don't copy their mascot). Secondary characters by prop: bank officer (tie),
  debt collector (phone headset), landlord (keys), judge (gavel), Mom (glasses, scarf).
- **One metaphor per episode** (like their audition), returning at start, middle and end.
- **Real objects personified, but generic:** a credit card with eyes, the credit-score gauge
  with a face, an envelope that gets angrier. YouTube logo is fine for commentary;
  avoid real bank, Visa/Mastercard or government logos (write names as text instead).
- **Our additions:** a live money counter in the corner, the world tint shifting
  green → orange → red as finances get worse, and a day timeline along the bottom.
- **Cost:** built fully in code (Remotion SVG), so **no image or video credits**.
  Only the narration costs credits (~8,000 with one take).
