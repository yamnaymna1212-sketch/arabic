## Description
What really happens if you stop paying your credit card? We follow Tyler from one missed
minimum payment to a late fee, a credit-score drop, a penalty rate, a charge-off, a debt buyer
who paid pennies for his account, and finally a knock on the door: a lawsuit.
Then the story splits into two endings: ignore it, or fight back.
