## Description
What happens if you just stop paying your car loan? Do they come in the middle of the night? Can the police get involved? And once the car is gone, is the debt gone too?

We follow Tyler, 27, from Columbus, Ohio, from one missed payment all the way to the end: late fees, credit damage, the 3 AM repo, the auction, a $9,500 bill for a truck he no longer has, a lawsuit, wage garnishment, and one twist almost nobody sees coming.
